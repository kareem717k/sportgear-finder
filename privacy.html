Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

SportGear Finder is a static informational website. There are no user accounts and no contact forms, and we do not ask you for any personal information. We use Google Analytics to understand which pages people find useful — see below.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure traffic — which pages are visited, roughly where visitors come from, and which links are clicked. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes your IP address (Google truncates IP addresses before storing them). We use this only to decide what to write about next; we never attempt to identify individual visitors, and we do not sell or share this data. You can opt out entirely with Google's browser add-on, or by blocking cookies for this site. See Google's Privacy Policy for how they handle the data.

Affiliate links

This site contains affiliate links to Amazon, Walmart, and Decathlon. If you click a link and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products we believe are genuinely useful. Affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial rankings.

Third-party services

Product and category images are served from Unsplash CDN and Amazon CDN. These services may set their own cookies or collect request data per their own privacy policies. We have no control over third-party data practices.

GitHub Pages hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect server access logs (IP addresses, request timestamps) as part of standard hosting operations. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us through the GitHub repository for this project.
