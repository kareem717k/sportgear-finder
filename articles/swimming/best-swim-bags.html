Best Swim Bags (2026)

Updated August 2026 · 6 min read · Affiliate links may earn us a commission

Home / Swimming / Best Swim Bags (2026)

A swim bag has one job a normal backpack can't do: carry soaked gear and dry clothes in the same bag without the dry things losing. That means a real wet/dry compartment, materials that shrug off puddled pool decks, and ventilation so your suit doesn't ferment between sessions.

There are two kinds of "swim bag," and most swimmers eventually own both: the mesh bag ($10–12) that holds your fins, paddles, and kickboard on deck, and the swim backpack ($55–80) that carries everything to and from the pool. Here are the best of each.

How to Choose a Swim Bag

Wet/dry separation is the whole point

Look for a dedicated waterproof-lined compartment — usually at the base or side — sized for a soaked suit and towel. Bags that offer only a flimsy internal pouch leave your dry clothes damp by the drive home. All four backpacks above do this properly.

Size to your swimming life

25–35L: lap swimmers and commuters. 35–40L: daily club training. 45L+: meets, doubles days, swim parents carrying for two. Oversizing sounds harmless but a half-empty 50L bag is a floppy nuisance on a bike.

Ventilation and materials

Mesh panels or drainage grommets in the wet zones prevent the chlorine-mildew smell that kills bags. Base fabric should be waterproof or at least heavily coated — pool decks are wet and locker room floors are worse.

The Bottom Line

Buy the TYR Alliance if you want the best all-round swim backpack, the Teamster 2.0 if you carry serious kit, and add the $12 Speedo mesh bag regardless — it's the highest value-per-dollar item in swimming.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a swim bag different from a regular backpack?

Wet/dry separation, drainage and ventilation, and water-resistant materials. A regular backpack keeps your wet suit sealed against your dry clothes and starts smelling of chlorine within a month.

Do I need a mesh bag and a backpack?

Most regular swimmers end up with both. The mesh bag holds in-water gear on deck and drip-dries it at home; the backpack handles transport and dry items. The mesh bag rides inside the backpack on the way in.

How do I stop my swim bag from smelling?

Empty it the moment you get home — wet compartments are for transport, not storage. Hang the bag open, rinse the wet pocket weekly, and dry mesh gear outside the bag.
