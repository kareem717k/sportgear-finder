Why these exist

Most gear "calculators" online are lead capture forms that ask for an email before showing a number. These are not. Every one of them runs entirely in your browser, shows the full reasoning behind the answer, and tells you when the cheap option is the right one.

Where a tool recommends a product category we link to our own comparison pages, which do contain affiliate links. The recommendations the calculators produce are not influenced by that — the sizing logic is standard coaching guidance, and in several cases it points you at the cheaper choice.
